Food Safety Tips

Eat Smart While Travelling

Follow these tips to enjoy local food safely and responsibly.

🌊

Choose Busy Stalls

Popular stalls with high turnover are fresher and safer than quiet ones.

💧

Drink Bottled Water

Always drink sealed bottled water. Avoid ice unless you know the source.

🧼

Wash Hands

Always wash hands before eating, especially after visiting markets and temples.

🌶️

Spice Levels

Indian food can be very spicy. Ask for mild preparations if you are not used to spices.

🥗

Fresh is Best

Prefer freshly cooked hot food over pre-cooked or reheated items at street stalls.

🍃

Eco-Friendly Eating

Choose establishments that use banana leaves, earthen pots or recyclable containers.
