Highlighted Destination Tips

Safety Tips

Protect Your Belongings

Keep valuables secure in crowded places. Use money belts or inside pockets for cash and cards.

Emergency Contacts

Save police (100), ambulance (108), fire brigade (101) and local emergency numbers before travelling.

Travel in Groups

Especially for remote or unfamiliar areas, travelling with others adds safety and convenience.

Stay Connected

Keep your phone charged and share your itinerary with a trusted family member or friend.

Know Your Area

Study the destination map before visiting. Download offline maps for areas with poor connectivity.

Nearest Medical Facility

Identify the nearest hospital or clinic at your destination before starting outdoor activities.

Responsible Travel Tips

Travel responsibly. Protect nature and respect communities.

Carry a reusable water bottle and avoid single-use plastics.

Never litter. Carry a small bag for your waste.

Do not damage or remove flora and fauna from natural reserves.

Do not disturb or feed wild animals.

Support local artisans and small businesses.

Do not use harmful chemicals near water bodies or coral reefs.
